How to Use Order App

Role-based workflow guide — your role: {{ user.role }}

{% if user.role == 'admin' %}

Admin Instructions

1 Manage User Accounts

Click Manage Users on the dashboard to open the admin panel. From there you can:

  • Create users — fill in username, password, and select a role (customer / sales / designer), then click "Create User".
  • Delete users — click the red "Delete" button next to any non-admin user.
  • Change roles — use the dropdown in the Role column to switch a user's role. Changes save automatically.
  • Show passwords — click "Show" next to the dots to reveal a user's password.

2 Customer → Sales Assignment

In the admin panel, use the "Customer → Sales Assignment" table to set a default sales person for each customer. When that customer creates a new order, the assigned sales person will be auto-selected (the customer can still override).

3 Monitor the Dashboard

The dashboard has three columns showing every order in the system:

  • Pending — orders actively being worked on.
  • Approved — customer-approved orders ready for production. Check the box and click "→ Production" to move them.
  • In Production — finalized orders. Expand to download drawings.
{% elif user.role == 'customer' %}

Customer Instructions

1 Create a New Order

From the dashboard, click "+ New Order" to open the order form. On the work page:

  • Enter your order details, specifications, and requirements in the left panel text area.
  • Upload any reference images, PDFs, or files using the "Upload Images / Files" button.
  • Select a Sales person from the dropdown (or leave the pre-selected default).
  • Click "Submit & Translate" — your text will be auto-translated to Simplified Chinese.

2 Wait for Drawings

After submission, the sales person reviews and approves the translation, then assigns it to a designer. The designer creates drawings based on your requirements. You can monitor your order's status in the Pending column on the dashboard.

3 Review and Approve Drawings

When drawings are ready for your review, the order appears in your Pending column with status "Customer Reviewing". Open the order and:

  • View the drawings in the right panel.
  • Approve — marks the order as approved and moves it to the Approved column.
  • Return to Sales — sends back to the sales person with your comments (e.g., revision requests). The workflow loops back for re-translation and re-design.

4 Update Requirements (if needed)

If you need to update your original requirements at any point while the order is in "Customer Input" status, you can edit the text in the left panel and click "Resubmit & Re-translate".

5 Download Final Drawings

Once your order is approved, the drawings become downloadable. Access them from the work page (right panel) or from the Approved / In Production columns on the dashboard by clicking the expand arrow.

{% elif user.role == 'sales' %}

Sales Instructions

1 Find Orders in Pending

When a customer submits an order and assigns it to you, it appears in the Pending column on your dashboard. Click any order to open the work page.

2 Review & Approve Translation

On the work page, the center panel shows the LLM translation into Simplified Chinese. You can:

  • Revise the translation directly in the text area if needed.
  • Assign a Designer from the dropdown.
  • Click "Approve Translation" — this sends the order to the designer.

3 Review Drawings

When a designer submits drawings, the order appears in your Pending column with status "Sales Reviewing Drawings". Open it and:

  • Approve Drawings — sends drawings to the customer for final review.
  • Return to Designer — sends back to the designer with your comments for revision.

4 Customer Review & Production

After you approve the drawings, the customer reviews them. If approved, the order moves to the Approved column. From there, check the box and click "→ Production" to finalize the order.

{% elif user.role == 'designer' %}

Designer Instructions

1 Find Assigned Orders

When sales approves a translation and assigns it to you, the order appears in the Pending column on your dashboard. Click the order to open the work page.

2 Review the Translation

On the work page, the center panel shows the approved Simplified Chinese translation. This is what you should base your drawings on.

3 Upload Drawings

In the right panel (Designer Drawings):

  • Click "Upload Drawings" and select your files.
  • All file formats supported. Max file size: 10 MB.
  • Delete any drawing by clicking the × button on the thumbnail.
  • You can upload and delete repeatedly until you're satisfied.

4 Submit for Review

Once all drawings are uploaded, click "Submit Drawings for Review". This sends the order back to sales for approval. If sales returns the drawings with comments, you'll see the comments in the status bar and can upload revised drawings.
